# Hypothesis

## Claim

When BTC perpetual funding is extremely positive, longs are crowded and
leveraged; the perp tends to mean-revert against them over the following days.

## Mechanism

Funding is a forced cash transfer from longs to shorts every 8 hours. At extreme
positive funding, marginal longs are paying to hold and are the most likely to be
liquidated or to capitulate. The flow is mechanical, not sentiment-driven.
