# Decision

## Context

What was true when this was decided? Link the evidence: experiments, learnings, prior
decisions. Cite artifacts by path rather than restating their numbers.

## Decision

What was decided, in the active voice. One paragraph.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is now foreclosed.

## What would reverse this

The condition under which this decision should be revisited. A decision with no reversal
condition is a belief.
